Your curiosity. Your little corner.

This is a first look at an academic biology workshop finder made for Sara. Every listing, date, location, and price is fictional — there’s nothing to apply for just yet.

  1. Explore a few possibilities.
    Try the sample filters and open a workshop that catches your eye.
  2. Keep your favorite ideas close.
    Tap the heart on any card. Saved finds stay in this browser when storage is available.
  3. Make the search yours.
    Research topics, methods, places, dates, and funding can take shape once you know what you’re looking for.

Later, a search will add real academic biology workshops here so you can compare your options. For now, every listing is a sample.
